# 热爱

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** rè'ài  
**English:** to love deeply; to be passionate about  
**Thai:** รักอย่างมาก / หลงใหล / มีใจรักอย่างมาก

## Character breakdown

[[热]] — rè — hot; warm; passionate  
[[爱]] — ài — love; to love

**热 + 爱 → 热爱**

## Meaning

热爱 means **to love something deeply or to be passionate about it**.

It expresses stronger enthusiasm and passion than [[爱]] in many contexts. It is commonly used for things such as one's work, country, life, profession, art, or an activity.

## Example sentence

**我热爱音乐。**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ rè'ài yīnyuè.  
**English:** I love music deeply. / I'm passionate about music.  
**Thai:** ฉันรักและหลงใหลในดนตรี
