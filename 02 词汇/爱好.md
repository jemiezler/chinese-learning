# 爱好

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** àihào  
**English:** hobby; interest; to be fond of  
**Thai:** งานอดิเรก / ความชอบ / ชื่นชอบ

## Character breakdown

[[爱]] — ài — love; to love  
[[好]] — hào — to like; to be fond of

**爱 + 好 → 爱好**

## Meaning

爱好 is commonly used as a **noun meaning "hobby" or "interest."**

It can also be used as a **verb meaning "to be fond of" or "to have an interest in."**

Note: In 爱好, [[好]] is pronounced **hào**, not **hǎo**.

## Example sentence

**我的爱好是听音乐。**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ de àihào shì tīng yīnyuè.  
**English:** My hobby is listening to music.  
**Thai:** งานอดิเรกของฉันคือการฟังเพลง
