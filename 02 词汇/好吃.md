# 好吃

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** hǎochī  
**English:** delicious; tasty; good to eat  
**Thai:** อร่อย / รสชาติดี

## Character breakdown

[[好]] — hǎo — good; well  
[[吃]] — chī — to eat

**好 + 吃 → 好吃**

## Meaning

好吃 means **delicious or tasty** and is used to describe food.

Literally, it has the sense of something being **good to eat**.

Note that [[好]] is pronounced **hǎo** in 好吃.

## Example sentence

**这个菜很好吃。**

**Pinyin:** Zhège cài hěn hǎochī.  
**English:** This dish is delicious.  
**Thai:** อาหารจานนี้อร่อยมาก
