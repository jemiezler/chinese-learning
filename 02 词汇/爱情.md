# 爱情

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** àiqíng
**English:** love; romantic love
**Thai:** ความรักเชิงโรแมนติก

## Character breakdown

[[爱]] — ài — love; to love  
[[情]] — qíng — feeling; emotion

**爱 + 情 → 爱情**

## Meaning

爱情 refers specifically to **romantic love**, especially love between romantic partners.

It is more specific than [[爱]], which can describe love more generally, such as love for family, people, activities, or things.

## Example sentence

**我相信爱情。**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ xiāngxìn àiqíng.  
**English:** I believe in love.  
**Thai:** ฉันเชื่อในความรัก
