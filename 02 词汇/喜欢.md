# 喜欢

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** xǐhuan  
**English:** to like; to enjoy; to be fond of  
**Thai:** ชอบ / ชื่นชอบ

## Character breakdown

[[喜]] — xǐ — joy; happiness; delight  
[[欢]] — huān — joy; happiness; pleased

**喜 + 欢 → 喜欢**

## Meaning

喜欢 means **to like, enjoy, or be fond of someone or something**.

It is an extremely common everyday word for expressing likes and preferences.

Compared with related words:

- [[喜欢]] — everyday **like**
- [[喜爱]] — to be fond of; slightly more formal
- [[爱]] — to love
- [[热爱]] — to love deeply; be passionate about

## Example sentence

**我喜欢学中文。**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ xǐhuan xué Zhōngwén.  
**English:** I like learning Chinese.  
**Thai:** ฉันชอบเรียนภาษาจีน
