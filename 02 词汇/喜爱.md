# 喜爱

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** xǐ'ài  
**English:** to like; to love; to be fond of  
**Thai:** ชื่นชอบ / โปรดปราน / รัก

## Character breakdown

[[喜]] — xǐ — joy; happiness; to like  
[[爱]] — ài — love; to love

**喜 + 爱 → 喜爱**

## Meaning

喜爱 means **to like or be fond of someone or something**.

It expresses affection or strong liking. It is similar to [[喜欢]], but 喜爱 can sound slightly more formal or literary.

## Example sentence

**我很喜爱中国文化。**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ hěn xǐ'ài Zhōngguó wénhuà.  
**English:** I really like Chinese culture.  
**Thai:** ฉันชื่นชอบวัฒนธรรมจีนมาก
