# 爱人

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** àiren  
**English:** spouse; partner  
**Thai:** คู่สมรส / คู่ชีวิต

## Character breakdown

[[爱]] — ài — love; to love  
[[人]] — rén — person

**爱 + 人 → 爱人**

## Meaning

爱人 refers to a person's **spouse or partner**.

In Mainland Chinese, it is commonly used for **husband or wife**.

## Example sentence

**他是我的爱人。**

**Pinyin:** Tā shì wǒ de àiren.  
**English:** He is my spouse/partner.  
**Thai:** เขาคือคู่ชีวิตของฉัน
