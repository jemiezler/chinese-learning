# 人

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** rén  
**English:** person; people; human  
**Thai:** คน / มนุษย์

## Core meaning

人 means **person, human, or people**.

It is one of the most common Chinese characters and frequently appears in words describing people, relationships, nationality, and occupations.

## Related vocabulary

- [[爱人]] — àiren — spouse; partner
- [[家人]] — jiārén — family member(s)
- [[别人]] — biérén — other people; someone else
- [[个人]] — gèrén — individual; personal
- [[主人]] — zhǔrén — host; owner
- [[中国人]] — Zhōngguórén — Chinese person

## Example sentence

**他是一个很好的人。**

**Pinyin:** Tā shì yí ge hěn hǎo de rén.  
**English:** He is a very good person.  
**Thai:** เขาเป็นคนที่ดีมาก
