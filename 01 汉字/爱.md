# 爱

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** ài  
**English:** love; to love  
**Thai:** รัก / ความรัก

## Core meaning

爱 means **to love, to care for, or to be fond of someone or something**.

It can express love for people as well as a strong liking for activities or things.

## Related vocabulary

- [[爱情]] — àiqíng — romantic love
- [[爱人]] — àiren — spouse; partner
- [[爱好]] — àihào — hobby; interest
- [[可爱]] — kě'ài — cute; lovable
- [[热爱]] — rè'ài — to love deeply; be passionate about
- [[喜爱]] — xǐ'ài — to like; be fond of

## Example sentence

**我爱我的家人。**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ ài wǒ de jiārén.  
**English:** I love my family.  
**Thai:** ฉันรักครอบครัวของฉัน
