# 情

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** qíng  
**English:** feeling; emotion; affection  
**Thai:** ความรู้สึก / อารมณ์ / ความผูกพัน

## Core meaning

情 refers to **feelings, emotions, affection, or emotional relationships**.

It often appears in words related to feelings, love, mood, and emotional situations.

## Related vocabulary

- [[爱情]] — àiqíng — romantic love
- [[感情]] — gǎnqíng — feelings; affection; relationship
- [[心情]] — xīnqíng — mood
- [[情况]] — qíngkuàng — situation; circumstances
- [[热情]] — rèqíng — enthusiastic; warm
- [[友情]] — yǒuqíng — friendship

## Example sentence

**我今天心情很好。**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ jīntiān xīnqíng hěn hǎo.  
**English:** I'm in a very good mood today.  
**Thai:** วันนี้ฉันอารมณ์ดีมาก
