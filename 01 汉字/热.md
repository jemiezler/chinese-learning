# 热

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** rè  
**English:** hot; warm; heated  
**Thai:** ร้อน / ร้อนแรง

## Core meaning

热 means **hot or warm**.

It can describe physical temperature, but it can also express **enthusiasm, passion, or popularity** in compound words.

## Related vocabulary

- [[热爱]] — rè'ài — to love deeply; to be passionate about
- [[热情]] — rèqíng — enthusiastic; warm; passionate
- [[热水]] — rèshuǐ — hot water
- [[天气热]] — tiānqì rè — the weather is hot
- [[热门]] — rèmén — popular; trending
- [[热心]] — rèxīn — enthusiastic; warm-hearted

## Example sentence

**今天很热。**

**Pinyin:** Jīntiān hěn rè.  
**English:** It's very hot today.  
**Thai:** วันนี้ร้อนมาก
