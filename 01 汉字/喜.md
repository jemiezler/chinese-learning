# 喜

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** xǐ  
**English:** joy; happiness; delight; to like  
**Thai:** ความยินดี / ความสุข / ชอบ

## Core meaning

喜 expresses **joy, happiness, delight, or liking something**.

It often appears in words connected with happiness, celebration, preference, and affection.

## Related vocabulary

- [[喜爱]] — xǐ'ài — to like; to be fond of
- [[喜欢]] — xǐhuan — to like
- [[喜悦]] — xǐyuè — joy; delight
- [[喜事]] — xǐshì — happy occasion
- [[惊喜]] — jīngxǐ — pleasant surprise
- [[喜庆]] — xǐqìng — festive; joyful

## Example sentence

**我喜欢学中文。**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ xǐhuan xué Zhōngwén.  
**English:** I like learning Chinese.  
**Thai:** ฉันชอบเรียนภาษาจีน
