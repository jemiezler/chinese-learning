# 欢

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** huān  
**English:** joy; happy; pleased  
**Thai:** ความยินดี / ความสุข / ร่าเริง

## Core meaning

欢 expresses **joy, happiness, pleasure, or a cheerful feeling**.

In modern Mandarin, 欢 is more commonly found as part of compound words rather than used by itself.

## Related vocabulary

- [[喜欢]] — xǐhuan — to like; to enjoy
- [[欢迎]] — huānyíng — to welcome
- [[欢乐]] — huānlè — happy; joyful
- [[欢喜]] — huānxǐ — joyful; delighted
- [[欢快]] — huānkuài — cheerful; lively
- [[欢笑]] — huānxiào — to laugh happily

## Example sentence

**欢迎你！**

**Pinyin:** Huānyíng nǐ!  
**English:** Welcome!  
**Thai:** ยินดีต้อนรับ!
