# 好

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** hǎo / hào  
**English:** good; well / to like; to be fond of  
**Thai:** ดี / ชอบ; โปรดปราน

## Core meaning

好 has two important pronunciations and meanings:

**hǎo** — good; well; nice  
This is the most common pronunciation.

**hào** — to like; to be fond of; to have a tendency toward something  
This pronunciation appears in words such as [[爱好]].

## Related vocabulary

- [[爱好]] — àihào — hobby; interest
- [[好吃]] — hǎochī — delicious
- [[好看]] — hǎokàn — good-looking; nice to look at
- [[好人]] — hǎorén — good person
- [[好朋友]] — hǎo péngyou — good friend
- [[友好]] — yǒuhǎo — friendly

## Example sentence

**他是一个好人。**

**Pinyin:** Tā shì yí ge hǎorén.  
**English:** He is a good person.  
**Thai:** เขาเป็นคนดี
