# 可

## Basic information

**Pinyin:** kě  
**English:** can; may; possible; suitable  
**Thai:** สามารถ / ได้ / เป็นไปได้

## Core meaning

可 generally expresses the idea of **possibility, ability, permission, or being suitable/acceptable**.

It often appears as part of compound words rather than being used alone in everyday speech.

## Related vocabulary

- [[可爱]] — kě'ài — cute; lovable
- [[可以]] — kěyǐ — can; may
- [[可能]] — kěnéng — possible; probably
- [[可是]] — kěshì — but; however
- [[可怕]] — kěpà — scary; frightening
- [[可惜]] — kěxī — unfortunately; what a pity

## Example sentence

**我可以进去吗？**

**Pinyin:** Wǒ kěyǐ jìnqù ma?  
**English:** May I go in? / Can I go in?  
**Thai:** ฉันเข้าไปได้ไหม
